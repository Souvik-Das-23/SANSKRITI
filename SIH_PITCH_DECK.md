## 💡 SLIDE 2: IDEA TITLE & PROPOSED SOLUTION

### Idea Title: **SANSKRITI (Cultural Tourism & Heritage Ecosystem)**

#### 1. Detailed Explanation of the Proposed Solution:
* **Interactive 3D Spatial Heritage & Kaalchakra Time-Travel**: 360° rotatable lightweight (<5MB) 3D GLB monument wireframes with an interactive split-slider restoring ruins to their 12th-century golden era state.
* **Sanskriti Parampara (Living Traditions Vault)**: Interactive digital archive of 8 classical dance forms & mudras, ancient martial arts (Kalaripayattu), stepwell hydro-engineering, and resonant acoustic Vedic chants.
* **Veda AI 2.0 & AI Heritage Lens**: Government of India Bhashini NLTM voice translation in 12+ Indic languages combined with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash sub-second multimodal vision artifact scanner.
* **Kala Bazaar (ONDC Seller Node)**: Direct fair-trade digital marketplace connecting travelers directly with verified rural artisan cooperatives with 0% middleman exploitation.
* **Sanskriti Yatra & Polygon PoS Passport**: Gamified zone-wise cultural passport with tamper-proof blockchain-verified digital certificates and GI-tag provenance seals.
* **Off-Grid Pilgrim SOS Safety Radar**: 100% offline GPS positioning and automated background SMS distress relay to **112** (Police) and **1363** (Incredible India Tourist Helpline).

#### 2. How It Addresses the Problem:
* **Solves Fragmented History**: Replaces disjointed commercial blogs with authentic, verified Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) monographs.
* **Preserves Intangible Heritage (ICH)**: Actively digitizes oral Vedic chanting frequencies, dance mudras, and metallurgy alongside stone monuments.
* **Eliminates Artisan Exploitation**: Bypasses middlemen siphoning up to 70% of artisan profits by routing orders through ONDC open rails.
* **Eliminates Remote Hazards**: Provides an off-grid safety net for travelers in cellular blackout areas (remote caves, high-altitude shrines).

#### 3. Innovation and Uniqueness of the Solution:
* **Dual-Mesh AR Temporal Shaders**: Real-time cross-fade comparison between ruined archaeology and reconstructed historical splendor.
* **Sovereign Indic Voice Stack**: Native dialectical voice guidance across 12+ languages via Bhashini NLTM, eliminating literacy barriers.
* **Zero-Gas Soulbound Token Provenance**: Polygon PoS blockchain smart contracts guaranteeing authenticity for Geographical Indication (GI) tagged crafts.
