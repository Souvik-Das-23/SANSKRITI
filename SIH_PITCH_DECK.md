### SLIDE 1: Idea Title, Problem Statement & Proposed Solution Description

#### Header & Metadata:
* **Problem Statement ID**: 26197
* **Problem Statement Title**: Student Innovation — Ideas that showcase the rich cultural heritage and traditions of India
* **Organization**: AICTE / MIC (Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell)
* **Theme**: Heritage & Culture | **Category**: Software
* **Project Name**: **SANSKRITI** — Sovereign Civilizational Heritage & Cultural Tourism Ecosystem
* **Tagline**: *"Bridging 5,000 Years of Indian Civilizational Heritage with Sovereign Technologies: Bhashini Voice AI, Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision, Mappls PostGIS Spatial Radar, ONDC Artisan Guilds, and Polygon PoS Provenance."*

#### The Problem & Key Industry Gaps:
1. **Fragmented & Commercialized Cultural Information**: Historical chronicles, dynastic lineages, and architectural paradigms (Nagara, Dravidian, Vesara) are scattered across unverified commercial blogs with no authentic Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) validation.
2. **Neglect of Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH)**: Living traditions (8 classical dance forms and mudras, Vedic acoustic chanting, ancient metallurgy like rustless iron) lack engaging interactive digital preservation.
3. **Exploitation of Rural Master Artisans**: Middlemen siphon up to 70% of craft revenues, while tourists are exposed to counterfeit machine-made replicas of Geographical Indication (GI) tagged crafts.
4. **Disconnection of Youth & Global Travelers**: Static museum placards and text boards fail to engage Gen-Z audiences without 3D models or Augmented Reality time-travel reconstruction.
5. **Zero-Connectivity Safety Hazards**: High-risk remote rock-cut caves, ghats, and high-altitude pilgrimage routes experience complete cellular blackout during emergencies.

#### Proposed Solution & Core Innovation:
**Sanskriti** is a sovereign mobile ecosystem engineered with a **Royal Obsidian (`#0C0C10`) & Imperial Gold (`#D4AF37`)** visual design system:
* **3D Spatial Computing & Kaalchakra AR**: Sub-5MB lightweight 3D GLB monument exploration and Google ARCore dual-mesh time-travel restoration slider (Ruined state ⇄ 12th Century restored state).
* **Sanskriti Parampara (Living Traditions Vault)**: Interactive chronicles of 8 classical dance forms, mudras, ancient martial disciplines, stepwell hydro-engineering, and high-resonance acoustic Vedic chants.
* **Veda AI 2.0 & AI Heritage Lens**: Bhashini NLTM real-time Speech-to-Text and Text-to-Speech across 12+ Indic languages combined with Google Gemini 1.5 Flash sub-second multimodal vision artifact scanner (structured JSON output).
* **Kala Bazaar (ONDC Seller Node)**: Direct fair-trade marketplace connecting travelers directly with verified rural artisan cooperatives with zero middleman exploitation.
* **Sanskriti Yatra & Polygon PoS Passport**: Gamified zone-wise passport (6 cultural zones) with tamper-proof blockchain-verified digital certificates and GI-tag provenance seals.
* **Off-Grid Pilgrim SOS Beacon**: 100% offline GPS positioning and automated background SMS distress relay to **112** (National Emergency) and **1363** (Incredible India Tourist Helpline).
